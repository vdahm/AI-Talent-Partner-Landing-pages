What exactly is inside an enriched profile? +
A structured candidate profile can include role and career context, Fit to Brief, external proof points and sources, available email and phone details, caveats, sourcing history and the feedback that explains why the profile was retained.
How does the sourcing loop work? +
Each batch creates new information. Human feedback, evidence and exclusions are retained as search context, previously surfaced candidates are deduplicated, and the next batch is net-new and more precise. Good candidates can move forward while sourcing continues.

THE 3D CANDIDATE VIEW

Three questions. Three different signals.

Instead of collapsing everything into one score, the ATS can keep the three questions separate and transparent.

01 / FIT

Does this person fit the role?

Fit to the real context, responsibilities and constraints - refined by recruiter feedback, not only keywords.

02 / PROOF

What supports that conclusion?

Independent evidence, company context and external sources strengthen or challenge what the profile claims.

03 / HUMAN SIGNAL

Are they ready to talk?

Motivation, communication, expectations, availability and role understanding come from direct human interaction.

Enriched profile = Fit + ProofUseful for the customer's own recruiting team.
Interview-ready candidate = Fit + Proof + Human SignalVerified by a recruiter before handoff.
