3D SOURCING

One sourcing process. Three connected research dimensions.

3D AI Sourcing is the research architecture. The candidate is only the first dimension. The process also checks what actually happened around the candidate and what independent evidence and market context support the conclusion.

3D AI Sourcing Explained ->
01 / CANDIDATE

Who is the person?

Career, roles, responsibilities, achievements and what the profile itself claims. This creates the first working view, not final proof.

02 / COMPANY + EVENT

What actually happened?

Company situation, business events, transactions, restructuring, growth, decline or other context during the relevant period.

03 / MARKET + EVIDENCE

What can be proven?

Independent sources, market map, feeder companies, geography and evidence that strengthen, qualify or challenge the candidate story.

Candidate is not proof. Proof is not market context. The ATS can preserve all three layers instead of collapsing them into one opaque match score.
HUMAN-GOVERNED AGENTIC RECRUITING

Controlled autonomy - with human gates where judgement matters.

The goal is not to put a human behind every AI action. It is to let agents work autonomously between defined checkpoints, then turn each human decision into both quality control and learning for the next iteration.

Human-Governed Agentic Recruiting Explained ->
01 / UNDERSTAND
Role + business contextAI structures the first Sourcing Profile from the role, market and relevant ATS history.
HUMAN GATE 1Search calibration
02 / SOURCE
Agentic 3D AI SourcingSearch, research, dedupe and candidate-batch creation across Candidate, Company/Event and Market/Evidence.
HUMAN GATE 2Candidate + evidence calibration
03 / RE-SEARCH
Continuous learningPositive anchors, negative anchors, hard exclusions and evidence requirements drive a net-new search.
HUMAN GATE 3Proof check
04 / ENGAGE
Personalised outreachAI prepares email, LinkedIn, voice, video, follow-ups, sequence and timing.
HUMAN GATE 4First-touch approval
05 / INTERACT
Replies + human signalAI classifies routine replies. Meaningful interest, questions or sensitive situations move to a recruiter.
HUMAN GATE 5Meaningful interaction
06 / QUALIFY
Verify + scheduleAI prepares the conversation and administration. The recruiter owns qualitative judgement; AI schedules and updates the ATS.
HUMAN GATE 6Candidate verification
Every human checkpoint is a Quality Gate + Learning Event. Feedback changes the Sourcing Profile, evidence requirements, messaging and the next search - instead of disappearing into a one-off chat.

3D CANDIDATE VALIDATION

Research architecture and candidate decision are two different 3D models.

3D AI Sourcing explains how the market and candidate are researched. 3D Candidate Validation explains how the final candidate outcome is assessed before it enters the customer's hiring process.

01 / FIT

Does this person fit?

Fit to the real role context, responsibilities and constraints - refined by recruiter feedback, not only keywords.

02 / PROOF

What supports the claim?

Independent evidence, company context, sources and caveats show how strongly the candidate story is corroborated.

03 / HUMAN SIGNAL

Are they actually ready?

Motivation, communication, expectations, availability, constraints and willingness to move come from direct human interaction.

3D AI SourcingCandidate -> Company/Event -> Market/Evidence.
3D Candidate ValidationFit -> Proof -> Human Signal. Human Signal is recruiter judgement, not an AI personality score.
