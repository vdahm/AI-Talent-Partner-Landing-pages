THREE HIRING MODELS

Same outcome. Different search depth.

The market determines how much sourcing depth, evidence and human work is needed per candidate.

SCALE HIRING

High Volume AI Sourcing

For repeatable roles where reach and personalised activation matter most.

EXAMPLE
Sales Representative rolesBroad market coverage with human-approved outreach and the same 3D verification standard.
  • Broad candidate discovery
  • Fast feedback loops
  • Personalised activation at scale
How does scale stay personal? +
Automation increases reach, but communication remains personalised. Candidate messages, voice notes and video can be prepared at scale, while a recruiter reviews the outreach before it is sent and handles the human conversation that follows.
PROFESSIONAL HIRING

Mid Volume AI Sourcing

For specialist and professional roles where context matters more than title matching.

EXAMPLE
IT Engineer rolesMore market calibration, deeper evidence and more context around each candidate.
  • Context-based search
  • Evidence enrichment
  • Targeted personalised outreach
How is the candidate market calibrated? +
The search starts broad. Human accept/reject feedback, role context and evidence from the market refine the next batch. A Candidate Market Map then estimates the reachable talent pool and the search depth that is realistic for the role.
HARD-TO-FIND HIRING

Highly Skilled / Executive Search

For small, complex markets where proof and human judgement need the greatest depth.

EXAMPLE
C-Level turnaround / restructuringDeep research, proof points and individual human verification for every serious candidate.
  • Market intelligence before search
  • Deep evidence research
  • High-touch candidate engagement
What changes in a hard-to-find market? +
The market is smaller, so each candidate receives deeper research. Fit is tested against the real brief, claims are strengthened with external proof points, and recruiter-led engagement becomes more individual before a candidate is considered ready for interview.
THE 3D CANDIDATE VIEW

Three questions. Three different signals.

Quality is easier to understand when fit, evidence and human interaction stay visible as separate signals instead of disappearing inside one score.

01 / FIT

Does this person fit the role?

Fit to the real context, responsibilities and constraints - refined by feedback, not only keywords.

02 / PROOF

What supports that conclusion?

External evidence, company context and public sources strengthen or challenge what a profile says about itself.

03 / HUMAN SIGNAL

Are they ready to talk?

Motivation, communication, expectations, availability and role understanding come from direct human interaction.

AI Sourcing builds Fit + ProofSearch, feedback and evidence enrichment create a stronger candidate picture.
Human verification completes the 3D viewThe third signal comes from a real conversation, not an AI personality score.
AI SOURCING IS THE METHOD

Simple on the surface. Iterative underneath.

The first view stays simple. Detailed search logic, market mapping and iteration rules can open only when someone wants to go deeper.

01
Start with the rolePlain-language role context is enough to begin.
02
Search + human feedbackCandidate batches improve as people accept, reject and explain why.
03
Enrich + verify evidenceStrong profiles gain independent proof points and available contact information.
04
Continue to the outcomeKeep sourcing - or move good candidates into personalised human verification.
The loop keeps learning: every new batch can use feedback, evidence and market information from the previous round. Good candidates can move forward while the search continues.
How does the Candidate Market Map work?See how reachable talent volume and search depth are assessed.
+
The Candidate Market Map estimates the reachable talent pool after the first calibration rounds. It uses role context, feedback, geography, feeder companies, evidence and market availability to determine whether the search behaves like scale, professional or hard-to-find hiring.
How do feedback loops improve the next batch?See how accept / reject reasoning becomes search context.
+
Recruiters mark profiles as good, review or reject and explain why. Those decisions become sourcing context for the next round, so the search learns from human judgement instead of restarting from the original brief.
How does evidence enrichment work?See how external proof points reduce reliance on self-description.
+
Strong profiles are researched beyond self-description. Public web sources and data providers are used to add or challenge proof points around achievements, company context, responsibilities and other relevant evidence.
